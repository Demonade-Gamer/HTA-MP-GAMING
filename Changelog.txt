FRANCE/NETHERLANDS CHANGES
	- France has been broken up into 3 nations, releasing Orleans and Champagne from the previous France
		- Orleans has ~645k pops, it owns half of Orleans and all of Loire state
		- Champagne is Ile de France, and the provinces of Laons and Chalons a little over 756k pops

		- It has Gent, Flanders, and Zeeland states, 1 million pops

	- Wallonia now has Charleville and Kimbre again, leaving it with 538k pops
